SADROG

Screen, window, camera and audio recording with a track-based editor, for macOS.

Download 1.0.0

Requirements

macOS 15 or later, Apple Silicon. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Permissions

macOS asks for Screen Recording when you record the screen, Microphone and Camera only when you start those sources.

Trial and activation

Opening, editing and saving projects needs no activation. Recording and export need activation: a 5-day trial, approved by the author and started at the first activation, or a permanent activation after a 25 EUR contribution (one Mac, works offline).

Source and support

Open source (GPL-3.0-or-later): github.com/gordasgdc/sadrog. Issues and questions go there.
