The Manager treats them differently because they behave differently on your computer.

Discover products without hunting for each one.

The catalog loads when you open the app. Browse by section — plugins by type, apps, materials, courses, events, partner stores — and filter by system and by type of access.

Offline, the app shows the last saved catalog and says plainly that it is not the current one.

Install, detect, update.

Plugins: installed where they belong

Pick a product and press Install. The Manager writes the files into the right Resolve folder and asks for the administrator password only if a direct write is refused.

Resolve loads plugins at launch, so the app warns you when it is open.

Updates, no surprises

Plugins update with one click. For apps, the Manager shows a new-version badge and an Update button that opens the product page. If the app is running, it tells you to quit it before installing.

Apps: recognised even if you installed them yourself

The My Apps page lists the GDC apps found on this Mac, including those downloaded and installed directly, without the Manager. Recognition uses the app’s identity and Apple signature, not the folder name; a test copy or a mounted disk image is not mistaken for your install.

You see the installed version, whether the app is running right now and whether a newer one exists. Installed does not mean activated: the licence stays in the app. The Manager does not uninstall apps it did not install.

This is in the Mac Manager, from version 1.41.0. Older versions do not have it; update from within the app.

Questions and support.

Does the Manager cost anything?

No. The app is free and needs no account. Products that need a licence are activated separately, through a donation or a granted right; amounts do not appear on this page.

I installed SADROG straight from the DMG. Does the Manager see it?

On Mac, yes, from version 1.41.0: it appears in My Apps with its installed version. The Manager does not install it, uninstall it or change its licence.

Why is an installed app missing from My Apps?

The Manager recognises known GDC apps by their identity and signature. A modified copy, a test copy or one on a mounted disk image is not recognised.

Does installing a plugin require restarting Resolve?

Yes. DaVinci Resolve reads plugins at launch; the Manager warns you if it is open.
